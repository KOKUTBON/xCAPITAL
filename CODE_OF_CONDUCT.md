# Code of Conduct

This policy applies to participation in the xCAPITAL repository and its community spaces.

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the xCAPITAL community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct incidents privately to the maintainers. The private security/contact options described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) may be used when no separate community contact is available. Include only the information necessary to understand the incident. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as reasonably possible and will avoid conflicts of interest when selecting a reviewer.

Enforcement responses may include a private warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces, depending on context and severity.
